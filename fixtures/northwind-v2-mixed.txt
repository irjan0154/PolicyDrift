Northwind Notes Privacy Policy

What we collect: We collect your email address, the notes you write and basic usage statistics such as how often you open the app.

Retention: We keep your data for 30 days after you close your account and then delete it.

Sharing: We may share your personal data with analytics providers and other third parties.

Purposes: We use your data only to run and improve the Northwind Notes service.

Your rights: You can export or delete your data at any time from the account settings.

Security: All notes are encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never keep unencrypted copies.

Contact: Write to privacy@northwind-notes.example for any privacy question.
